# Areeba Fatima's Portfolio 

This repo hosts the code for my portfolio website. It is an attempt to show my work in a way that doesn't bore you. 

I have spent more than 5 years working in Pakistan as a fact-checker, researcher, investigative reporter, video producer, broadcast journalist, and business reporter. Jack of all, master of none?

That's what I felt like. So I decided to focus on being an investigative reporter. I felt like that's where I was always trying to get, through different media, navigating an industry that has consistently ranked in the lowest quartile of the Press Freedom Index. 

I always found myself looking for a data scientist to work with me on my investigations, and I realized this is an essential skillset for the work I want to do in life.

So I applied for a Master's degree in Data Journalism at Columbia University. At that time, I had a pretty decent career at home. I was working wih the country's only Third Party Fact Checking partner with Meta, I had just left an international affairs show I was hosting on the country's 3rd largest broadcast channel, and I was on my way to joining the most-watched one. 

In New York City, I got a chance to film an interview with mayoral candidate Zohran Mamdani, who would later go on to win the election. I reported on the election, interviewed the Republican candidate Curtis Sliwa, covered campus protests when ICE arrested a student, interviewed a mermaid scientist, worked on LLM classifications, learned how to build tools, worked as a volunteer researcher for the Committee to Protect Journalists (CPJ), built agentic workflows as a researcher at Columbia University's Open Journalism Futures Lab under the leadership of Jonathan Soma. 

All while learning how to build a new life from scratch thousands of miles away from home, without any familiar faces.

You are here, because I must have applied for a job, and you are now looking at my portfolio.
